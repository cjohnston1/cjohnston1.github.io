Connor Johnston

Ph.D. student · Industrial and Systems Engineering

My main research interests are in integer programming and nonconvex optimization, with a particular focus on disjunctive cutting-plane methods. I also study the fair allocation of indivisible goods.

I spent summer 2026 as a research intern at Sandia National Laboratories, working on cutting-plane methods in Pyomo.

I earned a B.S. in Applied and Computational Mathematics with Honors and a B.A. in Philosophy from Florida State University in 2023.
